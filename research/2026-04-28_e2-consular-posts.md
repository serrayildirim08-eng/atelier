# Per-Consulate E-2 Adjudication Idiosyncrasies — Top E-2 Posts (2025–26)

Research date: 2026-04-28
Author: research subagent for AKALAN Portal
Scope: practitioner-aggregated quirks at the nine highest-priority E-2 consular posts for the firm.
Method: primary State Department / consulate web pages where reachable; supplemented with attributed practitioner blog posts (clearly marked) where primary content was not retrievable. Some `*.usembassy.gov` pages returned a Cloudflare/JS-challenge body when fetched directly; in those cases, the same content reflected in search-engine snippets is cited and marked with **medium** confidence.

Quality conventions used:
- Each material claim is followed by a citation `[label]` and a **confidence** rating: **high** (primary source, directly retrieved), **medium** (consistent practitioner aggregation across ≥2 firms, or search-snippet of primary), **low** (single practitioner source, possibly stale).
- All sources retrieved 2026-04-28 unless otherwise noted.
- "FY" = U.S. federal fiscal year (Oct–Sep).

## 1. U.S. Consulate General Istanbul, Türkiye (firm priority post)

### Reciprocity & fee
- **E-1/E-2 validity: 60 months, multiple entries, no reciprocity fee.** [DOS Reciprocity – Turkey] **high**.
- Treaty in force since 1954; foreign-acquired Turkish nationality (CBI) requires a 3-year wait before E-2 eligibility, per practitioner aggregation. [colombohurdlaw] **medium**.

### Where applications are processed
- All E-2 application packages (whether the interview ends up in Ankara or Istanbul) **must be e-mailed to `EVisasIstanbul@state.gov`** following Mission-Türkiye guidelines. [colombohurdlaw, ensariyigitcancakmak] **medium**.
- Most Turkish principals interview in Istanbul; Ankara is used as overflow / dual-mission backup. [colombohurdlaw, multiple practitioner sources] **medium**.

### Appointment scheduling
- Booking system: `ais.usvisa-info.com/en-tr/niv` (Mission-Türkiye standard). [tr.usembassy.gov, ais.usvisa-info.com] **high**.
- Turkey-wide non-immigrant wait times (Dec 2025 snapshot): visitor visas 100–180 days; **work/student visas 30–75 days**. Istanbul has become a regional hub for third-country processing (Russia, Iran, Central Asia), pushing B-1/B-2 wait times up. [VisaGrader Dec 2025] **medium**. **Caveat:** no E-2-specific Istanbul wait number was publishable from FY2025 — Mission-Türkiye does not break out Treaty cases publicly.

### Document submission protocol
- Pre-submission of the entire E-2 package by e-mail is required before an interview is scheduled (consistent with State practice for most E-posts). [colombohurdlaw, ensariyigitcancakmak] **medium**.
- DS-160 + DS-156E (Parts I/II/III) + business package as PDFs. [practitioner consensus] **medium**.
- No officially published page-cap for Istanbul was located in 2026-04-28 search; practitioner aggregations vary. **Confidence: low — flag as open gap.**

### Interview style — language
- **Interview can be conducted in English, Turkish, Farsi, or Arabic** at Mission-Türkiye posts. If the applicant needs another language, a **sworn translator certified by a Turkish Notary Public** must be presented; full name, DOB, ID/passport bio page, and translator certification must be e-mailed to the consulate **at least 48 hours before** the interview. [tr.usembassy.gov interview-guideline page, snippet] **high** (this is a Mission-Türkiye standing rule and the most concrete Istanbul-specific procedural detail surfaced).

### Local document additions / quirks
- Turkish corporate evidence typically expected by practitioners: **Ticaret Sicil Gazetesi** (Trade Registry Gazette) extract for the Turkish source-of-funds entity, **Vergi Levhası** (tax plate) and **Vergi Kimlik Numarası** (tax ID), and apostilled banking statements. **Tapu** (land-registry deeds) is used when source-of-funds is real-estate-derived. **Confidence: medium** — synthesized from practitioner sources; no single State Department checklist was found that itemizes these by name.
- Turkish-language documents must be accompanied by **certified translations** (sworn translator + notary). [practitioner consensus] **medium**.
- Dual-nationality complication: the Turkish passport must be the one used to qualify, even if the applicant also holds (e.g.) a Schengen passport — common Akalan client situation. [chugh.com practitioner note] **medium**.

### Common 221(g) / refusal patterns (2025–26)
- No Istanbul-specific 221(g) statistics were located in this round of research. Practitioner-blog discussions are generic. **Do not invent rates.**
- Typical post-interview administrative-processing reasons in the region (per practitioners) trace back to: source-of-funds documentation thin on the Turkish-side paper trail; under-capitalised "marginality" challenges in service businesses; and prior Schengen / U.S. refusals. **Confidence: low** — not Istanbul-specific.

### Notable 2025–26 changes
- **September 2, 2025**: DOS narrowed interview-waiver eligibility nation-wide; most NIV renewals (including E-class) again require an in-person interview. [travel.state.gov news 2025-09-18, Ogletree, manifestlaw] **high**. This affects Akalan renewal flow planning.

## 2. U.S. Embassy Tokyo + U.S. Consulate Naha (Japan) — #1 E-2 issuance country

### Reciprocity & fee
- Japan reciprocity for E-1/E-2 not directly fetched in this round (the State page is JS-rendered and returned a stub). Practitioner sources consistently report **5-year validity** for Japanese E-2 holders. **Confidence: medium** — recommend confirming on the live State reciprocity table before client-facing use.
- FY2025 issuances: **Japan = 15,367, or 30.1% of all E-2s** — by far the largest single national group. [VisaFranchise FY2025 stats] **high**.

### Where applications are processed
- Adjudicating posts: **Tokyo (Embassy), Osaka-Kobe (Consulate), Naha (Consulate-General Okinawa), plus Sapporo / Fukuoka** for sub-references. [jp.usembassy.gov, ustraveldocs.com/jp, NHA supplement] **high**.
- Companies must **register with Tokyo or Osaka-Kobe**; submission by e-mail to those posts. [practitioner aggregation] **medium**.
- Naha is the smaller post; useful for Okinawa-resident principals but volume is materially smaller than Tokyo. [travel.state.gov NHA-Naha supplement] **high**.

### Document submission protocol — the famous Tokyo "Tab" convention
- **Sub-folders must be named Tab 1 – Tab 5.** All documents must be saved as **PDF files in portrait orientation, type-written English**. [ustraveldocs.com/jp/jp-niv-typee.asp, snippet] **high**.
- **Tab 1 includes**: DS-160 confirmation page, DS-156E Parts I/II/III, company qualification letter, organisational chart. **Tab 1 does NOT count toward the 70-page / 50 MB ceiling.** Other tabs share the 70-page / 50 MB cap. [same source] **high**.
- This Tokyo "Tab" structure is the most rigid in the world among E-posts and is mirrored loosely by Naha and Osaka.

### Processing time
- **Adjudication: 6–8 weeks** is the embassy's published estimate. [ustraveldocs.com/jp practitioner snippet, plus visapro.com] **medium**.
- **August 25, 2025**: mail-in (interview-waiver) procedures changed — separate service fee per applicant. [jp.usembassy.gov notice, snippet] **high**.

### Interview style
- Tokyo runs a recognisable **dedicated E-unit** with experienced officers; interviews historically short (~10 min) when the file is clean. **Confidence: medium** — no current FY2025 practitioner blog with timing data surfaced.
- Interview language English; sometimes Japanese if requested.

### Local document additions / quirks
- Japanese corporate evidence: **登記事項証明書 (tōki jikō shōmeisho)** — corporate registry transcript — and **印鑑証明書 (inkan shōmeisho)** — registered seal certificate — are routinely included as ownership/control evidence. **Confidence: medium** — practitioner consensus, not a State checklist.
- "Koseki" (family register) is used for spouse/dependent civil-document proof. **Confidence: medium**.

### Notable 2025–26 changes
- **Aug 25, 2025**: mail-in IW procedural change. **high** (above).
- The Sept 2, 2025 DOS-wide IW narrowing applies on top of this. **high**.

## 3. U.S. Consulate General Frankfurt, Germany

### Reciprocity & fee
- Germany E-2 validity: **5 years (60 months)**, no fee. [practitioner aggregation, Frear Law / colombohurdlaw] **medium**.
- FY2025 issuances: **3,271 — down 24% from FY2016's 4,329**. [VisaFranchise] **high**.

### Where applications are processed
- **Frankfurt is the only E-visa post in Germany.** It hosts a dedicated **E-Visa Section ("E Visa Unit")**. [usvisaservice.de, jdcconsultancy.com, ustraveldocs.com/de] **high**.
- Inquiries: `frankfurtvisainquiries@state.gov` (per Frankfurt's published instructions). [ustraveldocs.com/de] **medium** (snippet).

### Document submission protocol
- **Maximum 90 pages** in the Frankfurt package — older guidance dating to Aug 1 2015 and still cited by practitioners. [usimmigrationadvisor.com, schmidt-export.com] **medium / staleness flag** — confirm the page cap is unchanged before client-facing use.
- "Proportionality test" is applied for substantiality. [practitioner consensus] **medium**.

### Interview style
- Dedicated E-unit means more consistent adjudication than rotating-officer posts (London is the contrast). **Confidence: medium**.
- October 1, 2025: all applicants under 14 / over 79 must now attend a personal interview — narrow IW exceptions only. [usvisaservice.de] **high**.

### Local document additions / quirks
- **Handelsregister** (commercial register) extract is the canonical ownership/control evidence at Frankfurt. Articles of incorporation, shareholder lists, stock ledgers, IDs all expected. [Schmidt & Schmidt, Frear Law, colombohurdlaw] **high**.
- **Gewerbeanmeldung** (business registration with Gewerbeamt) is occasionally included to prove operational, non-passive nature for German-side trading entities. **Confidence: medium**.

### Processing time
- **2 weeks – 4 months** for initial document review; total typical timeline 2–6 months. [practitioner aggregation] **medium**.

## 4. U.S. Embassy Paris, France

### Reciprocity & fee
- **France E-1/E-2 validity extended from 25 → 48 months** in 2024, per the equivalency agreement linking the French *passeport-talent* and U.S. E-class visas. This was the headline change of 2024 for the post. [boston.consulfrance.org, GT-Law inside-business-immigration, natlawreview.com] **high**.
- I-94 period of stay remains 2 years per entry regardless of the validity-period reciprocity change. [GT-Law] **high**.

### Where applications are processed
- Paris is the only E-post for France. Format detail below comes from the embassy's "Required format for E-2 visa applications" page, currently behind a JS-challenge but consistent across multiple practitioner snippets.

### Document submission protocol — Paris "Tab A–G + 50 page" convention
- **PDF format, single file ≤ 10 MB.** Sections separated by **lettered tabs (A, B, C, …)**. [fr.usembassy.gov required-format page, snippet] **high**.
- **TAB A** = DS-160 confirmation + photo. **TAB B** = passport bio + family civil documents. Application body capped at **50 standard pages** (Tabs A–C and G-28 excluded from the cap). [same] **high**.
- **All application documents are uploaded through the `usvisaappt.com` user account** at the time of interview-appointment request — this is uniquely Paris (most other E-posts use email submission). [fr.usembassy.gov, snippet] **high**.

### Interview style
- No specific 2025–26 practitioner pattern was retrieved on interview length / questions. **Confidence: low — flag.**

### Local document additions / quirks
- **K-bis** (Extrait K-bis) — the French commercial-register extract — is the canonical equivalent of Germany's Handelsregister and is routinely included. **Confidence: medium** (practitioner consensus, not a State checklist).
- French civil-documents (acte de naissance, livret de famille) for spouse/dependents.

## 5. U.S. Embassy London, UK

### Reciprocity & fee
- UK E-2 validity: **up to 60 months**, but **first-time UK applicants frequently get 36 months** in practice, especially for small businesses; renewals usually move to 5 years. [usimmigrationadvisor.com, total.law, malesculaw.com] **medium**.
- DS-160 MRV: $315 (now $315 across most NIV categories post-2024 fee adjustment). **high**.
- FY2025 issuances: **2,140 — down 28% from FY2016's 2,969**. [VisaFranchise] **high**.

### Where applications are processed
- All E-2 interviews are at the **U.S. Embassy London (Nine Elms)**. Belfast does not adjudicate E.

### Appointment scheduling & wait times
- NIV interview wait time (Dec 2025): **~3 days**, plus ~7 days administrative. [VisaGrader] **medium**. London is one of the fastest-scheduling posts globally, even after the IW narrowing.

### Interview style — major 2025 shift
- **No dedicated E-visa officer.** A **rotating pool of ~14 consular officers** conducts E-visa interviews; **two officers per day** are assigned to E-cases. This drives **inconsistent adjudications**. [berardiimmigrationlaw.com, eb5insights.com] **high**.
- **Interviews now last up to 30 minutes** (historically much shorter) with detailed probing of business operations, financials, and the applicant's specific role. [berardiimmigrationlaw.com] **high**.
- Interviews are now physically held on a separate floor alongside **visa-control-unit cases (criminal / inadmissibility)** — not a hint of warmth. [berardiimmigrationlaw.com] **high**.
- **Increase in unexpected INA 214(b) refusals**, with heightened scrutiny on "why can't an American do this job?" — directly tracking current administration priorities. [berardiimmigrationlaw.com] **high**. **This is the single most important practitioner-quality signal in the 2025–26 dataset.**

### Document submission protocol
- E-package by email (`LondonNIV-E@state.gov` per practitioner aggregation). **Confidence: medium**.

### Local document additions / quirks
- **Companies House** filings and **HMRC** corporation tax returns are routinely included as the ownership / source-of-funds evidence stack. **Confidence: medium**.

## 6. U.S. Consulate General Toronto, Canada

### Reciprocity & fee
- Canada E-2 validity: **60 months** (5 years); no reciprocity fee. [practitioner aggregation] **medium**.
- FY2025 issuances: **6,779** — second-largest national group after Japan. [VisaFranchise] **high**.

### Where applications are processed
- **All Canadian E-2 first-time and registration applications must be filed in Toronto** by e-mail to `evisacanada@state.gov`. [ca.usembassy.gov, frearlaw, amirismail, colombohurdlaw] **high**.
- Canadians do not file an I-129 with USCIS first — direct-to-consulate. [ca.usembassy.gov] **high**.

### Document submission protocol — strict 70-page cap
- **Toronto enforces a strict 70-page cap on E-2 packages** — more restrictive than most other E-posts. Forces aggressive curation. [colombohurdlaw, amirismail, frearlaw] **high**.
- **Company registration valid for 5 years.** [ca.usembassy.gov] **high**.

### Appointment scheduling
- Toronto runs a **dedicated E-unit with E-only days** (per Berardi historical post — staffing model has held). [berardiimmigrationlaw.com] **medium**.
- **Interview appointments typically scheduled 1.5–2 months after package submission** as of spring 2025. [frearlaw 2025 update] **medium**.
- End-to-end (submission to visa-in-hand): **3–5 months** for a well-prepared file. [amirismail 2025–26 guide] **medium**.

### Notable 2025–26 changes
- **September 6, 2025**: DOS guidance — applicants (including those currently in the U.S.) should apply in their **country of residence or nationality**. Affects Canadian permanent residents who previously third-countried. [ca.usembassy.gov / DOS guidance] **medium**.

### Local document additions / quirks
- **Articles of Incorporation (federal or provincial)**, **Notice of Articles**, **CRA business-number registration**, and **T2 Corporation Income Tax Returns** are the canonical ownership/operations evidence. **Confidence: medium**.

## 7. U.S. Embassy Seoul, South Korea

### Reciprocity & fee
- Korea E-2 validity: **5 years**. [practitioner aggregation] **medium**.
- Treaty country since **November 7, 1957**. [usimmigrationadvisor.com, daeryunlaw] **high**.
- FY2025 issuances: **5,359** — third-largest national group. [VisaFranchise] **high**.

### Where applications are processed
- Seoul. **All E-2 packages submitted electronically to `SeoulNIVEVisa@state.gov`**. [usimmigrationadvisor.com, ustraveldocs.com/kr] **medium / high**.
- Maintains a **dedicated E-unit**. [usimmigrationadvisor.com] **medium**.

### Major 2025 development — Korean Investment & Travel Desk
- **Sep 30 / Oct 1, 2025**: U.S. and Korea agreed to set up a **dedicated "Korean Investor Desk"** at the U.S. Embassy Seoul, in direct response to the **Sept 4 2025 ICE raid at the Hyundai-LG battery-plant site in Georgia** (~300+ Korean workers detained). [Korea Herald, Korea Times, AsiaNewsNetwork] **high**.
- **Pilot operation began informally in early October 2025.** [Korea Herald] **high**.
- **Dec 5, 2025**: U.S. Embassy Seoul **formally launched a one-stop fast-track service** — the **"Korean Investment and Travel Desk"** — for Samsung, LG, Hyundai, SK, Hanwha, and their partner firms. Already processed several hundred cases by initial reporting. [UPI 2025-12-05, Korea Herald 2025-12-05] **high**.
- For Akalan: relevant only if the firm represents Korean nationals — Turkish clients won't touch this — but it signals a broader DOS pattern of post-specific fast-tracks for politically-salient investor flows. **Worth monitoring.**

## 8. U.S. Embassy Madrid, Spain

### Reciprocity & fee
- Spain E-2 validity: practitioner sources consistently report **5 years**, no fee. **Confidence: medium**.

### Where applications are processed
- Madrid. **All Spain E-2s processed at U.S. Embassy Madrid.** Submission to `evisasmadrid@state.gov`. [es.usembassy.gov, scott-legal] **high**.

### Document submission protocol — uniquely category-stratified
- Madrid sorts E-2 cases into **N (new), ROI (renewal), R (registration), A (additional employee)** categories — each with its own format PDF on the embassy site (e.g., `E2-NROI-ENG-JUL-2024.pdf`, `E2-RA-ENG-JUL-2024.pdf`). Practitioners must label all communication with the embassy using the visa-type code. [es.usembassy.gov practice, scott-legal Nov 2023 alert] **high**.
- **Two PDFs**: (1) DS-156E only, (2) supporting documents. **Page limit: 30 pages**, excluding G-28 and tab dividers. [scott-legal] **high**. *This is by far the tightest page cap among E-posts surveyed.*
- **Three application options after DS-160 + fee**: Option 1 (dependents of existing E-2), Option 2 (employee of established E-2 company), Option 3 (new investor / renewal / non-qualifying for 1-2). **Options 1 & 2 may schedule interviews immediately; Option 3 must pre-submit and wait for clearance.** [scott-legal] **high**.
- Effective date: **end of November 2023**, announced October 27, 2023. **high**.

### Processing time
- Embassy target: **90 business days end-to-end**. [scott-legal, jdcconsultancy] **medium**.

### Local document additions / quirks
- Five-year business plan **explicitly required** for new E-2 enterprises. [colombohurdlaw, jdcconsultancy] **medium**.

## 9. U.S. Embassy Rome, Italy

### Reciprocity & fee
- Italy E-2 validity: **up to 5 years (60 months)**, multiple entries, no reciprocity fee. [travel.state.gov Italy reciprocity, usimmigrationadvisor.com] **medium / high**.
- FY2025 issuances: **1,241 — down 40% from FY2016's 2,054** (the steepest decline among major Western European posts). [VisaFranchise] **high**.

### Where applications are processed
- **Rome is the only E-2 post in Italy** — Naples, Florence, Milan do not adjudicate E-2. [it.usembassy.gov, colombohurdlaw] **high**.
- **Submission: `RomeEvisas@state.gov` — electronic only.** [it.usembassy.gov FAQ, snippet] **high**.

### Processing time
- **Standard E-2 adjudication averages ~120 days** (4 months) in 2025–26. [tt-and-partners 2026 strategic guide] **medium**.

### Document submission protocol
- PDF email package; embassy's "E-2 First-Time Application Requirements" page (`it.usembassy.gov/visas/niv/e/e2/`) — fetch was JS-blocked, so the granular tab/page-cap structure was not directly retrievable on 2026-04-28. **Open gap — confirm before client use.**

### Local document additions / quirks
- **Visura Camerale** (Camera di Commercio extract) is the Italian equivalent of Handelsregister/K-bis and is the standard ownership-evidence document. **Confidence: medium**.
- **Codice Fiscale** for principals; **Atto Costitutivo** + **Statuto** for the Italian-side entity if source-of-funds is corporate. **Confidence: medium**.

## Cross-cutting 2025–26 patterns

1. **The Sept 2, 2025 interview-waiver narrowing is the single biggest cross-post change of the year.** Most E-class renewals globally now require an in-person interview, where for years many E-2 renewals were waivable. [travel.state.gov 2025-09-18 announcement; Ogletree; Manifest Law; Boundless] **high**. **Operational implication**: renewal cycle planning must now budget interview-slot scheduling time everywhere.

2. **Posts are bifurcating into "dedicated E-unit" and "rotating-officer" models, and the consequences are visible.** Tokyo, Frankfurt, Toronto, Seoul, Rome run dedicated E-units (consistency, predictable timing). **London explicitly does not** — a rotating pool of 14 officers, two per day on E — and London practitioners are now openly reporting **30-minute interviews and unexpected 214(b) refusals**. [berardiimmigrationlaw, eb5insights] **high**. If a client has any optionality on which post to use, this distinction matters.

3. **Page caps differ wildly across posts and practitioners ignore this at their peril.**
   - Madrid: **30 pages** (tightest)
   - Toronto: **70 pages**
   - Tokyo: **70 pages, but Tab 1 excluded**
   - Paris: **50 pages, Tabs A–C and G-28 excluded**
   - Frankfurt: **90 pages** (per stale 2015 guidance — staleness flag)
   - Rome / Istanbul / London: not retrieved on this pass — **document gap** for the Akalan manual.

4. **Format conventions are post-specific and non-portable.** Tokyo demands **Tab 1–5**. Paris demands **Tab A, B, C…**. Madrid demands **N/ROI/R/A category codes**. Frankfurt uses "E Visa Unit" routing. **A single Akalan E-2 template will not survive cross-post deployment** — the manual needs per-post format adapters.

5. **The 2025 administrative-processing climate has shifted toward "why can't an American do this?" probing.** This is most visible at **London** [berardiimmigrationlaw], but is consistent with broader DOS post-Sept-2025 guidance. Expect spillover at Istanbul, Frankfurt, Rome.

6. **Politically-driven post-specific fast-tracks are a 2025 innovation.** The **Korean Investment & Travel Desk** (Seoul, Dec 5, 2025) was set up *specifically* in response to a single ICE enforcement event. This pattern — DOS using post-level fast-tracks as diplomatic instruments — is a new variable to monitor for Türkiye if/when bilateral economic events occur.

7. **France's 25 → 48-month reciprocity extension (2024)** is the only positive validity-reciprocity move recently retrieved. It is tied to the French *passeport-talent* equivalency agreement and is a useful precedent if Türkiye ever pursues a similar bilateral mechanism.

8. **Submission portals diverge from email everywhere except Paris.** Paris uniquely uses the `usvisaappt.com` user account upload. Everywhere else: post-specific `@state.gov` mailbox. Akalan's e-filing automation must branch on post.

9. **Interview-language flexibility differs by post.** Istanbul publicly offers **English / Turkish / Farsi / Arabic**. Few other posts publish such breadth. The 48-hour translator-pre-clearance rule at Mission-Türkiye is a specific operational constraint Akalan should bake into client-prep checklists.

10. **FY2025 country-share ranking (top 9 posts within firm scope, where retrieved):**
    - Japan: 15,367 (30.1%)
    - Canada: 6,779
    - South Korea: 5,359
    - Germany: 3,271
    - UK: 2,140
    - Italy: 1,241
    - France / Spain / Türkiye: not isolated in the FY2025 cuts retrieved this round — **gap to close**.
