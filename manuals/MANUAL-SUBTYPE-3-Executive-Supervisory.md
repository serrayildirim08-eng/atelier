# E-2 Manual — Subtype 3: Executive / Supervisory Employee

> **Status:** SKELETON. No firm exemplar yet OCR'd. Built from 9 FAM 402.9-7(2)(a)
> + 8 CFR §214.2(e)(17) + USCIS guidance. Will be calibrated against a firm
> filing in V2.

---

## 0. When this manual loads

Phase-0 detector returns `principal_subtype: 'executive_supervisory_employee'` when:

- "REQUESTED CLASSIFICATION: E2 — EXECUTIVE/SUPERVISORY" appears in cover
- Beneficiary's title is C-suite, VP, Director, GM, or equivalent
- Cover letter emphasizes P&L authority, signing authority, supervised teams
- CV documents prior executive roles with quantified P&L responsibility
- Org chart shows beneficiary at top tier with ≥3 direct reports
- Compensation matches or exceeds executive industry benchmark for the geography

---

## 1. Element checklist (9 FAM 402.9-7(2)(a) and 8 CFR §214.2(e)(17))

The treaty enterprise itself must already qualify as an E-2 entity. Then:

| Element | Authority | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Beneficiary's nationality matches enterprise's qualifying nationality | 9 FAM 402.9-7(2) | Passport |
| Position title | 9 FAM 402.9-7(2)(a) | Job description, org chart |
| Place in firm's organizational structure | 9 FAM 402.9-7(2)(a) | Org chart, board minutes |
| Duties: ultimate control or major-component supervision | 9 FAM 402.9-7(2)(a) | Job description, prior responsibilities |
| Number + skill levels of supervised employees | 9 FAM 402.9-7(2)(a) | Org chart, payroll, prior employer letters |
| Salary commensurate with executive role + industry + geography | 9 FAM 402.9-7(2)(a) | Job offer + compensation benchmark |
| Prior executive/supervisory experience | 9 FAM 402.9-7(2)(a) | CV + LoR + prior employer letters |
| Position is NOT primarily routine substantive staff work | 9 FAM 402.9-7(2)(a) | Drafter narrative + role description |

---

## 2. Anti-routine paragraph (CRITICAL DEFENSIVE)

Per 9 FAM 402.9-7(2)(a): if a position primarily involves routine work and only
secondarily supervises low-level employees, it FAILS this category. The drafter
MUST include a defensive paragraph asserting the executive nature of the role
with concrete examples — same pattern as the Subtype-1 anti-routine paragraph
in `MANUAL-SUBTYPE-1`'s develop-and-direct section.

Example template:

> "The Beneficiary's role is not limited to performing routine or hands-on
> operational tasks. Rather, his/her executive authority derives directly from
> [the corporate appointment / titled position] and is exercised through
> strategic oversight of [major component], managerial direction of [team /
> department], and control of [specific essential functions: budgets, hiring,
> vendor relationships, customer accounts, P&L]."

## 3. Tab structure (predicted)

Likely similar to Subtype 4 (Camural pattern) since both are employee filings:

```
A Cover Letter (likely Petitioner letterhead, possibly with attorney co-sign)
B Forms + Job Offer Letter
C Applicant Information — passport, visa, I-94, CV, prior exec experience proof, diploma, certificates, LoR
D Intent to Depart
E Nationality of the Investor (enterprise)
F Ownership / Corporate Structure
G Investment (enterprise's deployed capital)
H Real and Operating
[+ dependent tabs if applicable]
```

The DIFFERENCE from Subtype 4: Tab C and Tab H content emphasizes:
- Prior P&L responsibility (numbers + currency)
- Prior signing authority (contract values)
- Prior team size + skill levels supervised
- Prior major-component supervision (geography, product line, business unit)
- Org chart placement at top tier with span of control

## 5. Document inventory delta vs Subtype 4

Same baseline + emphasis on:

| New emphasis | Source |
|---|---|
| Prior P&L responsibility evidence | LoR from former employers, audited financials they signed off on |
| Prior signing authority | Sample contracts the Beneficiary executed (redacted), board resolutions delegating authority |
| Compensation benchmark for executive role | Industry salary survey + role's geography |
| Org chart with beneficiary at top tier | Updated US-entity org chart |
| Span-of-control narrative | Number of direct reports + indirect reports + total team size |

## 6. Authority allowlist (Subtype 3 specific)

- `INA §101(a)(15)(E)(ii)`
- `8 CFR §214.2(e)(17)` — exec/supervisory employees (PRIMARY)
- `8 CFR §214.2(e)(18)` — distinguishes from essential skills
- `9 FAM 402.9-7(2)(a)` — exec/supervisory standard (PRIMARY)
- `9 FAM 402.9-4(A)` — beneficiary nationality
- `9 FAM 402.9-4(B)` — enterprise nationality

## 7. Quality gates (predicted)

- [ ] Beneficiary's title is genuinely executive (not "Senior Specialist" or similar)
- [ ] Org chart shows beneficiary in top tier
- [ ] At least 3 documented direct reports (typical threshold)
- [ ] Compensation ≥ industry-25th-percentile for the executive role + geography
- [ ] At least one LoR from a prior employer attesting to executive duties
- [ ] Anti-routine paragraph present
- [ ] CV documents ≥ 5 years prior executive experience
