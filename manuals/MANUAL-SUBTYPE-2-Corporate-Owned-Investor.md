# E-2 Manual — Subtype 2: Corporate-Owned Treaty Investor

> **Status:** SKELETON. No firm exemplar yet OCR'd. Authority-grounded structure
> built from 9 FAM + 8 CFR. Will be calibrated against a firm filing in V2.
>
> Authority: `9 FAM 402.9-3`, `9 FAM 402.9-4(B)`, `8 CFR §214.2(e)(3)`.

---

## 0. When this manual loads

Phase-0 detector returns `principal_subtype: 'corporate_owned_investor'` when:

- The treaty investor IS a corporation (not an individual)
- The corporation is owned ≥ 50% by treaty-country nationals
- The individual beneficiary appears as the corporation's executive in the US
- Cover letter narrates the parent corporation's investment activity, not personal SOF

Often hard to distinguish from Subtype 4 (employee) at first glance. Key
differentiator: the **corporation itself holds the E-2 classification**, and
the beneficiary holds it derivatively as the corporation's exec — vs. Subtype 4,
where the corporation is the petitioner and the employee is the beneficiary.

## 2. Elements (per 9 FAM 402.9)

| Element | Subtype 1 differs in | Subtype 2 specific |
|---|---|---|
| Treaty country nationality | Individual's | Corporation's nationality + ≥50% owners' nationality |
| Substantial investment | Individual's funds at risk | Corporate funds at risk |
| Real & operating enterprise | Same | Same |
| Marginality | Same | Same |
| Develop & direct | Beneficiary individually | Beneficiary as corp's executive AND corp's strategic direction |

## 3. Document inventory (predicted)

Largely overlaps Subtype 1, but ADDS:

- Foreign corporation's articles of formation
- Foreign corporation's audited financials (recent years)
- Foreign corporation's shareholder register with nationality breakdown
- Board resolution authorizing the US investment
- Full ultimate-beneficial-owner (UBO) disclosure
- Corporate group org chart (parent → subsidiary)

---

## 4. Drafter notes

- Cover letter on FIRM letterhead (not Petitioner) — attorney as author.
- Roman-numeral sections (II–VIII), same Akalan convention as Subtype 1.
- Defined terms: `the Petitioner` (the US entity), `the Treaty Investor`
  (the foreign corporation), `the Beneficiary` (the individual).
- Distinct from Subtype 4: focus is on proving the CORPORATION's E-2
  qualification, not employee specialized knowledge.
