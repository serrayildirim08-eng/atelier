# E-2 Manual — Subtype 4: Essential Skills / Specialized Knowledge Employee

> Production manual for ConCistenC when the case is an **E-2 Essential Skills
> employee** sponsored by a qualifying treaty enterprise. Calibrated against
> the Camural / Pomega Energy filing (Feb 2024, 297 pages). Authority anchors:
> 8 CFR §214.2(e)(18); 9 FAM 402.9-7(2)(b).
>
> **Read order:** Master OS → `_E2-SUBTYPE-TAXONOMY.md` → this manual → case-specific intake.

---

## 0. When to load this manual

The Phase-0 case-subtype-detector loads THIS manual when the classifier returns:

```
{ principal_subtype: 'essential_skills_employee' }
```

Trigger signals (per taxonomy §7):
- "REQUESTED CLASSIFICATION: E2 — SPECIALIZED KNOWLEDGE" anywhere in cover
- Cover letter on **Petitioner / corporate letterhead** (not law firm)
- Cover letter signed by Petitioner officer (VP HR / COO), not attorney
- Job Offer Letter present in Tab B
- Beneficiary CV / Resume + Diploma + Certificates + Letters of Recommendation in Tab C
- Foreign + US entity corporate documents (treaty enterprise structure)
- Beneficiary salary stated with industry/peer comparison
- No I-539 dependent forms (or separately filed)

If any signal is missing or contradicts, route to attorney with the detection
report — do NOT auto-draft.

## 1. Tab structure (Akalan A–H pattern from Camural)

```
TAB A — TABLE OF CONTENTS AND COVER LETTER (combined)
TAB B — FORMS
        • G-1145 (e-Notification)
        • G-28 (Notice of Appearance)
        • I-129 (Petition for Nonimmigrant Worker)
        • I-129 E Supplement
        • G-1650 ACH Authorization (one per fee category)
        • Job Offer Letter
TAB C — APPLICANT INFORMATION
        • Passport (bio page)
        • US Visa stamp + I-94
        • Resume / CV
        • Beneficiary's Prior Work and Service Record
        • Diploma
        • Certificates (industry / training)
        • Letter(s) of Recommendation
TAB D — INTENT TO DEPART THE U.S.
        • Signed statement of intent to depart upon termination of E-2 status
TAB E — NATIONALITY OF THE INVESTOR (the treaty enterprise's owners)
        • Shares Certificate(s)
        • Passports of owners (Beneficiary's nationality must MATCH the qualifying nationality)
TAB F — OWNERSHIP
        • Foreign-entity corporate documents (Articles, board, public-listing if any)
        • Foreign-entity shares certificates
        • Passports of owners (foreign + US)
        • Organizational charts of BOTH entities (parent + subsidiary)
        • US-entity corporate documents (Articles, Operating Agreement, EIN)
        • US-entity shares certificates
TAB G — INVESTMENT (parent → subsidiary, NOT individual SOF)
        • Wire confirmation (parent's bank → US subsidiary's bank)
        • Recipient bank receipt
        • Balance Sheets (multi-period, showing capital deployment over time)
TAB H — REAL AND OPERATING
        • Project Catalogue / Product Catalogues
        • Customer / Offtake Agreements (long-term commitments)
        • Vendor equipment invoices (capital assets — assembly lines, production equipment)
        • Lease Agreement (commercial premises)
        • Land Purchase Agreement (if industrial / plant build-out)
        • Payroll Summary
        • Latest Profit & Loss Statement
        • Latest Balance Sheet
```

**No dependent tabs in Camural's filing** because the beneficiary entered solo
on a B-2. When dependents are present, append:

```
TAB I — Forms for Dependents (G-28, I-539, I-539A, G-1650)
TAB J — NOID for Dependents
TAB K — Biographic / Immigration Info for Dependents (passport, visa+I-94, vital records + translations)
```

## 2. Cover letter — Petitioner-corporate voice

The cover letter is **drafted by the Petitioner**, not the law firm. It must
ship on the Petitioner's corporate letterhead, signed by an officer (typically
VP Human Resources or COO).

### 2.1 Section structure (verbatim Camural / Pomega)

```
[Header: Petitioner letterhead, USCIS service center, RE block]

[Opening paragraph: parties, requested validity dates, beneficiary's
current immigration status, role title, brief bio of beneficiary]

[Petitioner intro paragraph: company name, structure, parent/subsidiary
relationship, industry, market position]

INVESTMENT, ESTABLISHMENT, OWNERSHIP AND CONTROL
DOING BUSINESS
PREMISES
OPERATING PERSONNEL
FINANCIAL SITUATION AND FUTURE PLANS
THE BENEFICIARY'S PROSPECTIVE ROLE
BENEFICIARY'S EMPLOYMENT HISTORY AND QUALIFICATIONS
CONCLUSION

[Signature block: Officer name, title, Petitioner legal name]
```

### 2.2 Mandatory content per section

#### INVESTMENT, ESTABLISHMENT, OWNERSHIP AND CONTROL
- Treaty-country nationality of enterprise's owners (≥ 50%)
- Parent → subsidiary corporate relationship
- Date of US subsidiary formation
- Initial investment amount + date + wire route
- Shareholder breakdown (Camural: Kontrolmatik TR 50%, Pomega TR 10%, Kontrolmatik US 7%, individual TR shareholders 32%, German 1% = ≥92% Turkish)
- Names + titles of executive leadership
- *Authority cite:* `INA §101(a)(15)(E)(ii)` and `9 FAM 402.9-4(B)`

#### DOING BUSINESS
- Industry, products, services
- Specific product lines
- Customer commitments / offtake agreements
- Capital equipment ownership / acquisition
- Inline `(Exhibit: …)` for each claim

#### PREMISES
- Operating address
- Lease agreement (commercial)
- For plant/facility builds: site selection narrative, land purchase

#### OPERATING PERSONNEL
- Current headcount (W-2 vs 1099 breakdown)
- Number of executives/managers vs total
- Reporting structure summary
- Projected headcount with growth narrative

#### FINANCIAL SITUATION AND FUTURE PLANS
- Capital deployment to date (verbatim figures with date stamps)
- Liquid assets, fixed assets, total worth
- Income statement summary
- 5-year strategic narrative (industry tailwinds, expansion plans, IRR/DSCR if known)
- Government incentives (PTC, IRA, state credits) where applicable
- *This section is the substantiality + marginality combined argument*

#### THE BENEFICIARY'S PROSPECTIVE ROLE
- Why the company NEEDS this role (commercial necessity narrative)
- Specific responsibilities (verbatim from Job Offer Letter)
- Reports-to structure (cite Org Chart)
- Required qualifications for the role
- Foreign-language requirements where applicable (Camural: English + Turkish for Pomega coordination)
- *This is where the "essential to operations" argument gets staged*

#### BENEFICIARY'S EMPLOYMENT HISTORY AND QUALIFICATIONS
- Education credentials
- Years of experience (≥ 10 typical)
- Specific employers + roles + responsibilities
- Geographic territories covered
- Quantified achievements (Camural: "doubled annual sales target in 2022 at SIEMENS")
- Key certifications + training
- LoR direct quote (italicized)
- Salary differential vs domestic peers — **CRITICAL** per 9 FAM 402.9-7(2)(b)
- Domestic salary in foreign country (TRY 140K vs TRY 45K average peer)
- US salary offered + benefits
- *Authority cite:* `9 FAM 402.9-7(2)(b)` and `8 CFR §214.2(e)(18)`

#### CONCLUSION
- One-sentence summary tying labor force + income potential to public interest
- Specific validity dates requested
- Officer signature block

### 2.3 Voice features (must be replicated by drafter)

| Feature | Pattern |
|---|---|
| Pronouns | Corporate `we` / `our` (not third-person attorney voice) |
| Tone | Professional but marketing-flavored — Petitioner is selling its operations to USCIS |
| Adjectives | Permitted: "engineering marvel", "ambitious undertaking", "industry-leading" |
| Politicians / public figures | Permitted when independently verifiable (e.g., "Senator Lindsey Graham has expressed interest") |
| Section heads | ALL CAPS, no Roman numerals |
| Exhibit citations | Inline `(Exhibit: <Title>)` parenthetical, integrated into prose |
| Currency | Always with comma + .00 (`$4,000,000.00` or `TRY 140,000.00`) |
| Company name | Verbatim legal name on first reference; short form thereafter |
| Letter-of-recommendation excerpts | Italicized direct quote with author attribution |
| Defensive paragraph: skills not US-available | Required per 8 CFR §214.2(e)(18) |
| Defensive paragraph: time-limited essentiality | Required when role is short-term / training-focused |

## 3. Per-document manual entries

### 3.1 Tab A — Cover Letter (drafter output, see §2 above)

### 3.2 Tab B documents

#### 3.2.1 Job Offer Letter — NEW skill required
- **What it is:** Petitioner-issued letter offering the role to the beneficiary, with title, start date, base salary, bonuses, benefits.
- **Tab/Exhibit:** B.1 (after standard forms). **APS:** 4.
- **Element proven:** Specialized-knowledge role anchor + salary attestation.
- **Why it matters:** Adjudicators verify role description + compensation against the cover letter and the I-129E.
- **Agent extraction schema:**
  ```json
  {
    "petitioner_legal_name": "<>",
    "officer_name": "<>",
    "officer_title": "<>",
    "beneficiary_name_ascii": "<>",
    "role_title": "<>",
    "reports_to_title": "<>",
    "base_salary_usd": <number>,
    "bonus_terms": "<verbatim>",
    "benefits": ["401k", "health", "dental", "vision", "life"],
    "start_date": "<YYYY-MM-DD>",
    "validity_or_offer_expiry": "<>",
    "signed_date": "<>"
  }
  ```
- **Skill:** `job-offer-extractor` (NEW).
- **Quality gate:** `base_salary_usd` must equal salary stated in cover letter "BENEFICIARY'S EMPLOYMENT HISTORY AND QUALIFICATIONS" section. Mismatch = severity 4.

#### 3.2.2 Form I-129 + I-129 E Supplement
- See main manual §1.3. Same logic. The I-129E for an employee filing carries the **petitioner enterprise's** investment / ownership, not a personal investment. Aggregator cross-checks must use the corporate values.

### 3.3 Tab C documents

#### 3.3.1 Passport, Visa, I-94
- See main manual §3.1, §3.2, §3.4.
- **Critical nuance:** Beneficiary's nationality MUST match the qualifying nationality of the treaty enterprise (the ≥50% owners). 9 FAM 402.9-7(2). Mismatch = severity 5.

#### 3.3.2 Resume / CV — extended schema
- **What it is:** Beneficiary's professional history.
- **Tab/Exhibit:** C.2. **APS:** 3 (self-prepared) but corroborated by Service Record + Diploma + Certificates.
- **Element proven:** Specialized knowledge — depth of experience.
- **Why it matters:** Specialized knowledge requires "degree of proven expertise" + "length of experience" (9 FAM 402.9-7(2)(b)).
- **Agent extraction schema:**
  ```json
  {
    "beneficiary_name_ascii": "<>",
    "education": [{"degree":"<>", "institution":"<>", "year":"<>", "country":"<>"}],
    "employment_history": [{
      "employer":"<>","title":"<>","start":"<YYYY-MM>","end":"<YYYY-MM>",
      "geographic_coverage":["<country>"],
      "responsibilities":"<>","quantified_achievements":"<>"
    }],
    "total_years_experience": <number>,
    "industry_specialization": "<>",
    "languages": [{"lang":"<>","level":"<native|fluent|professional>"}],
    "key_skills": ["<>"]
  }
  ```
- **Skill:** `cv-extractor` (existing — needs extension for `geographic_coverage`, `quantified_achievements`, `languages`).

#### 3.3.3 Prior Work and Service Record
- **What it is:** Foreign-country government / employer-issued employment record showing past salary.
- **Tab/Exhibit:** C.3. **APS:** 4-5 depending on issuer.
- **Element proven:** Salary differential proof for specialized-knowledge essentiality.
- **Why it matters:** "Salary the special qualifications can command" is an explicit factor under 9 FAM 402.9-7(2)(b). Camural showed TRY 140K/mo when Turkish average was TRY 45K — 3× peers.
- **Agent extraction schema:**
  ```json
  {
    "issuer_authority": "<e.g., SGK Service Record / Employer HR>",
    "issuer_country": "<>",
    "beneficiary_name_ascii": "<>",
    "employer_history": [{"employer":"<>","start":"<>","end":"<>","most_recent_salary":{"amount":<>,"currency":"<ISO>","frequency":"monthly|annual"}}],
    "country_average_salary_for_role": {"amount":<>,"currency":"<>","source":"<>"},
    "salary_differential_multiple": <number>
  }
  ```
- **Skill:** `service-record-extractor` (NEW).

#### 3.3.4 Diploma
- **What it is:** University degree certificate.
- **Tab/Exhibit:** C.4. **APS:** 5 (institution-issued).
- **Element proven:** Education credential supporting specialized knowledge.
- **Agent extraction:** institution, degree, field, conferral date.
- **Skill:** `credential-extractor` (NEW; subsumes Diploma + Certificates + EAD if present).
- **Quality gate:** if degree is from a non-US institution, ensure foreign-credential evaluation accompanies (or note absence).

#### 3.3.5 Certificates
- **What it is:** Industry training, certifications relevant to the specialized role.
- **Tab/Exhibit:** C.5. **APS:** 3-4.
- **Element proven:** Specialized knowledge breadth.
- **Agent extraction:** issuer, certification name, date earned, expiration if any.
- **Skill:** `credential-extractor` (extended).

#### 3.3.6 Letter(s) of Recommendation
- **What it is:** Third-party (former employer, senior peer, customer) endorsement letter.
- **Tab/Exhibit:** C.6. **APS:** 3-4 (depends on author seniority).
- **Element proven:** Specialized knowledge — independent corroboration.
- **Why it matters:** Adjudicators give weight to senior third-party endorsements that quote specific achievements.
- **Agent extraction schema:**
  ```json
  {
    "author_full_name": "<>",
    "author_title": "<>",
    "author_employer": "<>",
    "author_relationship_to_beneficiary": "<former_manager|peer|customer|other>",
    "letter_date": "<YYYY-MM-DD>",
    "endorsement_quote_verbatim": "<>",
    "specific_achievements_cited": ["<>"]
  }
  ```
- **Skill:** `recommendation-letter-extractor` (NEW).
- **Drafter use:** the strongest verbatim quote should be inserted into the cover-letter "BENEFICIARY'S EMPLOYMENT HISTORY AND QUALIFICATIONS" section, italicized, with author attribution.

### 3.4 Tab D — Notice of Intent to Depart
- See main manual §9.1. Same logic. Nonimmigrant intent for the beneficiary.

### 3.5 Tab E — Nationality of the Investor (the enterprise's qualifying nationality)
- **What it is:** Shares certificates + owners' passports proving ≥50% treaty-country nationality of the petitioner enterprise.
- **Tab/Exhibit:** E.1, E.2.
- **Element proven:** 9 FAM 402.9-4(B). The *enterprise* must qualify; only then can the employee be sponsored.
- **Skill:** `corporate-doc-extractor` + `passport-extractor` per owner.
- **Aggregator gate:** sum of treaty-country ownership ≥ 50% → severity 5 if not.

### 3.6 Tab F — Ownership (parent + subsidiary structure)

#### 3.6.1 Foreign-entity corporate documents
- **What it is:** Articles of formation, board resolutions, share register, public-listing docs (if any) for the foreign parent.
- **Tab/Exhibit:** F.1. **APS:** 5 if government / exchange-issued; 4 internal.
- **Element proven:** Existence + structure + nationality of the parent entity.
- **Why it matters:** The parent is the source of treaty-country qualifying nationality and (often) the source of investment.
- **Agent extraction schema:**
  ```json
  {
    "foreign_entity_legal_name": "<>",
    "foreign_country": "<>",
    "entity_type": "<JSC | LLC | LLP | publicly_listed_corporation>",
    "stock_exchange": "<if listed>",
    "exchange_ticker": "<>",
    "formation_date": "<>",
    "registered_office": "<>",
    "board_members": [{"name":"<>","role":"<>","nationality":"<>"}]
  }
  ```
- **Skill:** `foreign-corporate-doc-extractor` (NEW).

#### 3.6.2 US-entity corporate documents
- See main manual §4.1, §4.2. Standard Articles + Operating Agreement.

#### 3.6.3 Org charts (BOTH entities)
- **Tab/Exhibit:** F.4. **APS:** 3.
- **Element proven:** Reporting structure — beneficiary's place in the hierarchy + reports-to chain.
- **Skill:** `org-chart-extractor` (extended — multi-entity variant showing parent + subsidiary on same diagram).
- **Quality gate:** beneficiary's box must be present + connected to the executive-leadership tier.

### 3.7 Tab G — Investment (corporate funding model)

#### 3.7.1 Wire confirmation (parent → US subsidiary)
- **What it is:** Single international wire from parent's foreign bank to subsidiary's US bank.
- **Tab/Exhibit:** G.1. **APS:** 5.
- **Element proven:** Investment movement.
- **Why it matters:** This is the corporate analog of the Subtype-1 SOF chain. Must show: parent company name, foreign bank, foreign account, US recipient bank, US account, amount, value date.
- **Agent extraction schema (corporate variant):**
  ```json
  {
    "sender_entity": "<parent legal name>",
    "sender_bank": "<>",
    "sender_account_last4": "<>",
    "receiver_entity": "<US subsidiary legal name>",
    "receiver_bank": "<>",
    "receiver_account_last4": "<>",
    "amount_usd": <>,
    "value_date": "<YYYY-MM-DD>",
    "swift_reference": "<>",
    "wire_purpose_memo": "<verbatim>"
  }
  ```
- **Skill:** `wire-confirmation-extractor` (extended for corporate variant — no FX gate when both sides are USD).

#### 3.7.2 Recipient bank receipt
- **What it is:** US bank's record of the incoming wire.
- **Tab/Exhibit:** G.2. **APS:** 5.
- **Element proven:** Funds actually received in subsidiary's account.
- **Skill:** `bank-receipt-extractor`.

#### 3.7.3 Balance sheets (multi-period)
- **What it is:** Subsidiary's balance sheets at multiple snapshots showing capital deployment over time.
- **Tab/Exhibit:** G.3. **APS:** 3 (self-prepared).
- **Element proven:** At-risk + substantiality — funds were not parked, they were deployed.
- **Why it matters:** Camural showed Q2 2023 ($2.55M deployed) and Q4 2023 ($7.39M total fixed assets) — demonstrates active deployment.
- **Skill:** `financial-statement-extractor` (NEW).
- **Aggregator gate:** trace deployment trajectory; require ≥ one snapshot showing significant fixed-asset increase post-wire.

### 3.8 Tab H — Real and Operating

#### 3.8.1 Project / Product Catalogues
- **What it is:** Glossy documents describing the enterprise's products and capabilities.

- **Why it matters:** A signed offtake is the strongest possible non-financial proof of viability.
- **Agent extraction schema:**

#### 3.8.3 Vendor Equipment Invoices (capital assets)

## 4. Specialized-knowledge proof matrix

The drafter must populate every cell below before producing BENEFICIARY'S
EMPLOYMENT HISTORY AND QUALIFICATIONS. Empty cells = severity 4 escalation.

| Factor (9 FAM 402.9-7(2)(b)) | Source documents | Extracted via |
|---|---|---|
| Degree of proven expertise | CV + Diploma + Certificates + LoR | cv-extractor, credential-extractor, recommendation-letter-extractor |
| Whether others possess the skill | LoR + Industry context paragraph (drafter narrative) | recommendation-letter-extractor + drafter |
| Length of experience with enterprise (or in industry) | CV + Service Record | cv-extractor + service-record-extractor |
| Training period to perform the duties | CV + Job Offer Letter (if states required experience) | cv-extractor + job-offer-extractor |
| Skill's relationship to enterprise's processes | Job Offer Letter + Project Catalogue + Beneficiary CV alignment | drafter narrative |
| Salary the qualifications command | Service Record + Job Offer Letter + (industry benchmark from public source) | service-record-extractor + job-offer-extractor |
| Skills not readily available in US | Drafter narrative — must explicitly assert AND cite supporting facts | drafter |
| Foreign-language-and-culture insufficient | (Negative — drafter must NOT rest the case on language alone) | drafter compliance check |

## 5. Time-limited essentiality (drafter judgment)

Per 9 FAM 402.9-7(2)(b), some skills are essential only for short-term
training of locally hired employees, and some for the start-up phase only.
The drafter must position the case honestly:

| Pattern | Drafter framing | Validity to request |
|---|---|---|
| Plant build-out + employee training of US workforce | "essential during construction and ramp-up phase" | 2 years (typical Camural) |
| New product launch / quality control | "essential through commercial-scale production" | 2-3 years |
| Long-term ongoing role | "durable expertise unique to the enterprise's specific processes" | up to 5 years |

**Avoid:** characterizing the role as permanently essential when the facts
suggest training-then-handoff. Adjudicators flag overstated permanence.

- `9 FAM 402.9-4(B)` — enterprise nationality
- `9 FAM 402.9-4(F)` — nonimmigrant intent

- `9 FAM 402.9-7(2)(b)` — essential skills employees (PRIMARY)

DO NOT cite Matter of Walsh and Pollard or Matter of Ho here (those are
investor-substantiality cases, not employee cases).

## 7. Quality gates (Reviewer's Phase 4 checklist for Subtype 4)

- [ ] Petitioner letterhead (NOT firm) on cover letter.
- [ ] Cover letter signed by Petitioner officer (VP HR, COO, etc.).
- [ ] Beneficiary nationality matches the qualifying nationality of the enterprise.
- [ ] Treaty-country combined ownership of the enterprise ≥ 50%.
- [ ] Job Offer Letter present and consistent with cover letter role + salary.
- [ ] CV documents ≥ 5 years relevant industry experience (typical threshold).
- [ ] Service Record or equivalent shows salary differential vs domestic peers.
- [ ] Diploma + at least one Certificate or Letter of Recommendation.
- [ ] LoR is from a senior third-party (former manager, customer, peer with rank).
- [ ] Defensive paragraph "skills not readily available in U.S." present.
- [ ] If start-up / training role: time-limited essentiality framed.
- [ ] No language-alone reliance in specialized-knowledge argument.
- [ ] Investment chain: parent → subsidiary wire confirmed by both sender + receiver banks.
- [ ] Balance sheets show progression of deployment.
- [ ] At least one customer offtake / supply agreement OR strong revenue trajectory.
- [ ] All cited authorities are in the allowlist (§6 above).
- [ ] All filing-bound names ASCII; original spellings preserved in source quotes.
- [ ] All currency in ISO + commas + .00.
- [ ] Conflict register: no severity ≥ 4 unresolved.
