## 2. The 4-Axis Examination Framework

Every framework manual, case-strategy memo, or RFE-response analysis
the team produces follows the same four-axis review:

| Axis | Owner | Question | Deliverable per topic |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1. Regulatory** | Suárez-Lopez | What does the statute / regulation / FAM / Policy Manual *require*? Where does discretion live? | Doctrinal map: INA → 8 CFR → 22 CFR → 9 FAM → USCIS PM, with verbatim quotations from controlling text |
| **2. AAO + Case Law** | Yılmaz | What does the adjudicator actually *do* with this pattern, and what does recent AAO/BIA/federal practice show? | Cited-decisions table, denial-rate snapshot, controlling precedent identification |
| **3. Practical Execution** | Whitfield | How does a competent practitioner *assemble, file, defend* this matter? | Step-by-step methodology, packet structure, timeline, RFE-response patterns |
| **4. Edge Cases + Defensive Framework** | Demir | When does the modal pattern *break*, and what defensive framing handles the deviation? | Edge-case registry, defensive-paragraph templates, sub-type calibration rules, jurisdictional analogies |

The four axes are sequential in deliberation but parallel in drafting:
each attorney drafts independently from their lens, then the team
cross-reviews before consolidation.

## 3. Working Model

### 3.1 Cadence

| Phase | Duration | Activity |
|---|---|---|
| **Kickoff** | Day 0 | Supervisor (Serra) frames the topic, scope, and deadline. Team accepts. |
| **Independent drafting** | Days 1–7 | Each attorney drafts their axis section in isolation. No cross-talk during this phase to preserve independent perspective. |
| **Cross-review** | Days 8–10 | Each attorney reviews the other three drafts. Comments delivered as inline annotations + a 1-page summary letter per reviewer. |
| **Consolidation** | Days 11–13 | A rotating consolidator (rotates per project) integrates the four drafts + cross-review comments into a unified deliverable. |
| **Supervisor review** | Days 14–15 | Serra reads the consolidated draft. Either signs off or sends back with edits. |
| **Publication** | Day 16 | Final version committed to `manuals/` with a version stamp. Posted to the firm's internal repository. |

### 3.2 Delegation rules

- **Lead axis.** Each attorney owns their axis as primary author.
- **Supporting role.** Each attorney is also a cross-reviewer of the
  other three; they are expected to flag issues outside their primary
  axis only when material.
- **Consolidator rotation.** Suárez-Lopez (project 1) → Yılmaz (project
  2) → Whitfield (project 3) → Demir (project 4) → cycle. The
  consolidator does not get to override another attorney's axis-domain
  judgment without Supervisor sign-off.
- **Tie-break.** When two attorneys disagree on a substantive question,
  the Supervisor decides. The dissent is preserved in the deliverable's
  appendix as `Note: <Author> dissents on <issue>; the firm adopts the
  majority position pending further development.`

### 3.3 Supervisor interaction protocol

The Supervisor is the ultimate authority. The team produces work; the
Supervisor accepts, edits, or rejects.

- **Strategic framing.** Only the Supervisor sets scope and priority.
- **Substantive override.** The Supervisor may override any team
  recommendation; the dissent is logged.
- **Publication gate.** No deliverable ships without explicit
  Supervisor sign-off (initials + date).
- **Edge-case escalation.** Demir's edge-case registry escalates new
  patterns to the Supervisor; the Supervisor decides whether to add
  them to the framework or treat as one-off.

## 5. Active Project — E-2 Framework Module

### 5.1 Scope

The E-2 Framework Module synthesizes the firm's E-2 doctrine across the
four axes, building on:

- `manuals/E2-MANUAL-FOR-CLAUDE-CODE.md` (bot-facing, already shipped)
- `manuals/E2-PREPARATION-MANUAL.md` (practitioner-facing, already
  shipped)
- `manuals/_E2-SUBTYPE-TAXONOMY.md` (sub-type detection, already shipped)
- `manuals/MANUAL-SUBTYPE-1/2/3/4-*.md` (per-sub-type calibration)

The module supplements those with the **doctrinal foundation**, the
**adjudicator-behavior empirical layer**, and a **defensive-framework
catalog** the existing manuals do not yet carry.

### 5.2 Per-axis deliverables (E-2 module)

#### 5.2.1 Suárez-Lopez — Regulatory map

```
File: manuals/E2-FRAMEWORK-1-REGULATORY.md
Length: 6,000–9,000 words
Sections:
  1. Treaty foundation: INA § 101(a)(15)(E)(ii) — text, legislative history, 1952
     codification, post-IIRIRA amendments
  2. Implementing regulations: 8 CFR § 214.2(e) — section-by-section
  3. State Department guidance: 9 FAM 402.9 — full read with cross-section
     hierarchy (-4 nationality, -6 substantial / real / marginal,
     -7 develop-and-direct + employee variants)
  4. Consular regulations: 22 CFR § 41.51 — derivative dependents, MRV fee
  5. USCIS Policy Manual: Vol. 2, Pt. F — internal cross-references
  6. Treaty list status: which countries qualify, treaty type (FCN vs BIT vs
     other), accession dates, effect on filing eligibility
  7. Discretion mapping: which provisions are mandatory vs discretionary,
     which are subject to deference doctrines (Chevron / Skidmore /
     Loper Bright shifts post-2024)
  8. Recent regulatory action: any FRN / EOIR / USCIS guidance issued
     in the trailing 24 months
```

#### 5.2.2 Yılmaz — AAO + case law

```
File: manuals/E2-FRAMEWORK-2-AAO-CASELAW.md
Length: 5,000–8,000 words

  1. BIA precedent table:
     - Matter of Ho, 19 I&N Dec. 582 (BIA 1988) — business plan credibility
     - Matter of Walsh & Pollard, 20 I&N Dec. 60 (BIA 1988) — marginality

  2. AAO denial pattern empirical study: trailing 5 years, by sub-type,

  3. Federal court appeals: APA challenges to E-classification denials,
     mandamus posture, recent circuit-level decisions
  4. Adjudicator priors by service center (CSC vs VSC vs NSC vs TSC)
  5. Common AAO rationales: substantiality below threshold, marginality
```

## 8. Conventions

- **Native diacritics preserved** in all attorney voices and proper
  nouns ("Suárez-Lopez", "Yılmaz", "Çamural", "Beşiktaş",
  "Tapu Müdürlüğü"). ASCII fold only for kebab filenames in the
  technical pipeline.
- **Citations always primary.** "9 FAM 402.9-4(B)" — never "the
  immigration regs" or "USCIS guidance" without a pinned cite.
- **Currency convention** matches `manuals/E2-PREPARATION-MANUAL.md`
  (`<ISO> <amount>.<cents>`).
- **Date convention.** ISO 8601.
- **Roman numerals** for cover-letter sections; Tab letters for
  exhibits (per existing firm convention).
- **Length.** Each axis section in a module: target the words listed
  in § 5.2.x; under-deliver only with stated reason; over-deliver only
  with consolidator approval.
