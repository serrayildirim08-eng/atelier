## 9.1 ADDENDUM (post-OCR of Flatturbo NOID 1, NOID 2, Final Denial)

After OCR'ing the image-only USCIS docs from Flatturbo, the actual fail signatures supersede the ones derived from the cover-letter alone. The TRUE failure was:

1. **B-2 status violation + unauthorized employment** — Beksac on B-2 visitor visa from 2022-09 to 2023-03; case theory required him to have been operating the LLC since 2022-08-19. USCIS: "when you perform the day-to-day functions that are essential to the enterprise's chances of success, this would constitute unauthorized employment."
2. **Material change between RFE response (2023-12-26) and ITD response (2024-02-09)** — RFE said "operational since 2022-08-19"; ITD response said "did not engage in business activities until 2023". USCIS cited *Matter of Izummi* 22 I&N Dec. 169 + 5 other AAO/BIA cites that the case is fatal once material changes appear in response to USCIS pressure.
3. **External evidence contradiction (Yelp reviews)** — Petitioner claimed e-commerce only; USCIS independently pulled 3 Yelp reviews dated Jan 11, Jan 12, Feb 5, 2024 showing automotive repair services. This is a smoking-gun fact-finding move USCIS performs that Atelier's pre-filing reviewer must anticipate.
4. **Status gap of 6 months** — B-2 expired 2023-03-22; I-129 filed 2023-09-22. Petitioner was out of status at filing.

**New gates added to proposed list (revising § 6 above):**

| Proposed gate | Severity | Trigger | Action |
|---|---|---|---|
| `b2_status_violation_signal` | 5 | Petitioner currently on B-2/B-1/ESTA AND case theory asserts "fully operational" / "actively doing business" before E-2 work auth | BLOCK drafting |
| `status_gap_pre_filing` | 5 | `(filing_date - prior_status_expiration_date) > 0` (out of status at filing) | BLOCK; require consular pivot OR 8 CFR § 248.1(b) extraordinary-circumstances showing |
| `material_change_in_response_to_uscis` | 5 | Compare new factual narrative against initial filing; any material change to date / ownership / business-activity timeline / operational status flagged | Surface to attorney pre-filing; better to acknowledge deficiency than retroactively rewrite |
| `external_evidence_contradiction_risk` | 4 | Pre-filing: check Yelp / Google / BBB / social media / Wayback Machine / public-records of Petitioner's claimed business activities against the petition's claims | Surface any discrepancy pre-filing |

**Authority cites Atelier must NOT provoke** (i.e., must structure cases so these don't apply):
- *Matter of Bardouille*, 18 I&N Dec. 114 (BIA 1981)
- *Matter of Drigo*, 18 I&N Dec. 223 (BIA 1982)
- *Matter of Katigbak*, 14 I&N Dec. 45 (Reg. Comm'r 1971)
- *Matter of Izummi*, 22 I&N Dec. 169 (Assoc. Comm'r 1998)
- *Matter of Michelin Tire*, 17 I&N Dec. 248 (Reg. Comm'r 1978)
- *Matter of Soriano*, 19 I&N Dec. 764 (BIA 1988)

These are the "you cannot fix a deficient case after the fact" cites. If the case has a material defect at filing, Atelier must counsel the firm to **withdraw and refile**, not to bandage with a supplement.
