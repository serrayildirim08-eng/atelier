## 0. When this manual loads

Phase-0 detector returns `principal_subtype: 'individual_investor'` when:

- Beneficiary is the actual investor (owns ≥ 50% OR has operational control)
- Cover letter on **law-firm letterhead** with Roman-numeral sections
- Membership Interest Transfer Agreement OR Operating Agreement names beneficiary as ≥50% member
- Personal SOF chain narrated (property sale, rental income, savings, gift, inheritance, loan-non-collateralized-by-enterprise)
- I-129 + I-129 E Supplement filed under the LLC as petitioner

**Reference case:** Kacar-Salih (Wise Guys Deli Cumberland LLC, RI, $120K, Jan 2026, 426 pp).

## 4. Defensive drafting hooks (mandatory when applicable)

- **Foreign-jurisdiction title-deed transfer** (e.g., Turkish Tapu Müdürlüğü) — explain BEFORE exhibit
- **Indexed rent escalation** (TEFE/TUFE/CPI) — footnote explanation
- **Spouse-named lease** — household-resources defensive paragraph
- **No-impermissible-loans pre-emption** — required in IV.B
- **Anti-routine paragraph** in VII when title combines exec + skilled-worker (e.g., President + Executive Chef)
