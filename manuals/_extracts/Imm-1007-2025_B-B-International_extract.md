## Voice signatures harvested (Akalan RFE-response style)

This is a **second voice profile** distinct from the cover-letter voice in `_VOICE-CORPUS-from-Kacar-Salih.md`. Useful when Atelier drafts RFE/NOID responses (vs initial cover letters).

### Section-heading convention
- ALL CAPS, **thematic** (not Roman-numeral), descriptive of the legal point at issue.
- Examples: `TIMELY FILING OF FORM I-129 PRIOR TO EXPIRATION OF STATUS`; `PROTECTIVE FILING OF FORM I-539`; `BENEFICIARY HAS MAINTAINED LAWFUL STATUS`; `APPROVAL OF FORM I-539 IS NOT REQUIRED`.
- Each heading = a discrete legal claim; sub-arguments live as bullets within.

### Opening boilerplate (template)
> "This response is submitted on behalf of the Petitioner, **[PETITIONER LEGAL NAME]**, in response to the Request for Evidence dated **[RFE_DATE]**. Please note that a Form G-28, Notice of Entry of Appearance as Attorney, is enclosed with this response. (Exhibit: G-28, Notice of Entry of Appearance as Attorney)"

### Argument-paragraph mechanics (state → rule → apply → conclude)
> "The Beneficiary was admitted to the United States in **[STATUS]** with an authorized stay valid until **[DATE]**. (Exhibit I-94 Record; Exhibit: Passport and B-2 Visa)
>
> The Petitioner timely filed the Form I-129 petition requesting E-2 classification, which was received by USCIS on **[FILING_DATE]** (Exhibit: …).
>
> Accordingly, Form I-129 was properly filed before the expiration of the Beneficiary's authorized stay.
>
> Under applicable regulations and USCIS policy, a nonimmigrant who files a timely request for change of status is considered to be in a period of authorized stay while the petition is pending. See **[CFR_CITATION]**."

### Bullet-list recap pattern (used at the end of each thematic section)
> "Therefore, the evidence demonstrates that:
> • The Form I-129 was timely filed prior to expiration of status
> • The Beneficiary filed a protective Form I-539 to ensure continuity
> • The Beneficiary has remained in a period of authorized stay; and
> • The Beneficiary has fully maintained lawful nonimmigrant status."
