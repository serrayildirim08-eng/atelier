## Sub-type CORRECTION (canonical for manual update)

The folder is labeled `(BUS)` and the firm's intake-level taxonomy seems to default to Subtype 1 (`individual_investor`) for "BUS" cases. **This case is Subtype 3 (`executive_supervisory_employee`)**, not Subtype 1. The classification matters because:

| Sub-type | Treaty investor identity | Beneficiary's E-2 basis | Authority addenda |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 (individual_investor) | The Beneficiary | Owns ≥ 50% + develops/directs | 9 FAM 402.9-7(1) |
| 2 (corporate_owned_investor) | A treaty-country corporation | Beneficiary qualifies *via* the corporation's nationality but is themselves the principal investor | 9 FAM 402.9-4(B) |
| **3 (executive_supervisory_employee)** | **The corporate parent** | Beneficiary is an executive of the treaty enterprise (NOT the investor) | + 9 FAM 402.9-7(2)(a); 8 CFR § 214.2(e)(17) |

Cemre's case maps cleanly to Subtype 3:
- **Treaty investor:** Musluoglu İnşaat (Turkish corp), 100% owner of Musluoglu Global Inc.
- **Beneficiary:** Cemre Musluoglu, Director + Business Development Manager (executive role)
- **Funding chain:** Musluoglu İnşaat (Turkey) → Musluoglu Global (Florida); plus a small $9,000 personal contribution from Cemre that was a separate deposit (not investment under E-2 standards but a co-mingled transfer — RFE flagged this)

**Calibration finding for `_E2-SUBTYPE-TAXONOMY.md`:**
The Phase-0.6 detector should NOT use the SharePoint folder label `(BUS)` as a sub-type signal. It must read the cover letter's "Classification Sought" line + the I-129 E Supplement's investor identity to disambiguate Subtype 1 vs Subtype 3. Cemre's initial cover letter said "E2 (Principal Treaty Investor)" but the underlying facts were Subtype 3 — the *attorney's* initial classification was wrong, and the detector should not blindly trust it.

The strongest detector signals for Subtype 3:
- Cover letter or I-129 E Supplement names a **corporate** treaty investor (not the Beneficiary)
- Beneficiary is "appointed" to a role (member resolution, board action by the corp)
- Beneficiary's I-129 lists the petitioner as the US subsidiary, not the Beneficiary
- SOF chain runs through a corporate account (not personal)
- Beneficiary's nationality matches the corporate parent's controlling-shareholder nationality

## SOF chain (worked example for manual § 4)

This is the cleanest worked example seen so far for a **corporate-parent-funded** SOF chain (Subtype 3). The chain runs:

```
[MUSTAS Akaryakıt Ltd. (Turkey, sister company)]
   │
   │  3 board resolutions (2024-07-19, 08-12, 08-16) authorize total $90,000 profit distribution
   │  to Mr. Ibrahim Musluoglu (the controlling shareholder of MUSTAS)
   │
   │  ├─ 2024-07-27  $40,000  withdrawn from MUSTAS Is Bank USD account
   │  ├─ 2024-08-15  $20,000  withdrawn
   │  └─ 2024-08-19  $30,000  withdrawn
   │  ─────────────  ────────
   │                 $90,000  total distribution to Mr. Musluoglu
   │
   ▼
[Mr. Ibrahim Musluoglu (personal)]
   │
   │  2024-10-01  cash deposit of $90,000 → Musluoglu Insaat Is Bank USD account
   │
   ▼
[Musluoglu Insaat (Turkish parent of Musluoglu Global)]
   │
   │  ├─ 2024-11-05  Wire $30,000  → Musluoglu Global Chase account (received as $29,935 after fees)
   │  └─ 2025-01-29  Wire $50,000  → Musluoglu Global Chase account (received as $50,000)
   │  ─────────────  ────────
   │                 $80,000  total invested
   │
   ▼
[Musluoglu Global Inc. (Florida, US enterprise)]
```

**Manual § 4.1 five-link chain validation:** ALL FIVE LINKS PRESENT —
- Origin: MUSTAS operating revenues (corporate sister entity)
- Conversion: not needed (USD-denominated MUSTAS Is Bank account)
- International transfer: 2 SWIFT wires Musluoglu Insaat → Musluoglu Global
- US deposit: Chase Bank Musluoglu Global
- Deployment: investment in active operations (lease, payroll, inventory, marketing, etc.)

**Critical observation about the second installment cash-flow:** the $50,000 was actually a partial of a $69,000 distribution authorized on 2024-11-18. The withdrawal hit MUSTAS USD account on 2024-11-21; the deposit to Musluoglu Insaat on 2025-01-29 came in two same-day pieces ($40,000 + $10,000); then immediately wired to Musluoglu Global. This shows Akalan's discipline at **chain-of-custody narration with same-day reconciliation** — a high-bar voice signature.
