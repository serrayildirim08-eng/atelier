## How to use this module

- **Building a new E-2 case.** Start with Part 3 (intake protocol). When
  the case turns on a non-modal pattern, consult Part 4 (defensive
  paragraphs + edge-case registry). Validate the file at filing time
  against Part 5 § 3 (50-point diagnostic).
- **Responding to an RFE.** Start with Part 2 (denial-pattern
  empirical) to identify the canonical rationale the officer is
  applying, then Part 3 § 7 (RFE response craft). For unusual issues
  raised by the officer, cross-check Part 4.
- **Training a new attorney.** Read in order: Part 1 (regulatory
  foundation), Part 2 (case-law canon), Part 3 (practical
  methodology), Part 4 (edge cases). End with Part 5 § 1 (firm
  position summary).
- **Calibrating the bot.** This framework supplements
  `E2-MANUAL-FOR-CLAUDE-CODE.md` (bot-facing) and
  `E2-PREPARATION-MANUAL.md` (practitioner-facing). The framework
  carries the doctrinal foundation and the empirical layer those two
  manuals presuppose.
