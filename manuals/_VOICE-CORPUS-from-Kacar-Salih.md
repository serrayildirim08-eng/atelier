## Voice signatures (verbatim phrases from real filing)

### Section openings
> "The Beneficiary has committed to a total investment of $120,000.00, of
> which $80,000 has already been irrevocably placed at risk and which
> derives from lawful sources in Turkey…"

### Element-satisfaction wrap-up phrases
> "Accordingly, the Petitioner is properly and lawfully structured to
> support E-2 Treaty Investor classification."

> "establishes the requisite degree of control and authority required
> under INA §101(a)(15)(E) and 9 FAM 402.9 for an investor seeking
> admission to develop and direct a qualifying enterprise."

### Source-of-funds narration pattern
> "Under Turkish law and customary practice, real property transfers are
> effected directly through the Land Registry Office (Tapu Mudurlugu), and
> a separate written sales contract is not issued in standard title deed
> transfers. Accordingly, the lawful sale of the Beneficiary's real
> property is evidenced through the official title deed records."

> "These transfers were effected immediately following the title transfer
> and reflect the customary and lawful method of disbursement of real
> property sale proceeds under Turkish banking and land registry practice."

### Inline exhibit citation format
```
(Exhibit: Membership Interest Transfer Agreement dated December 5, 2025
— Ozlem Demir to Salih Kacar)

(Exhibit: Prior Turkish Title Deed dated January 25, 2013)

(Exhibit: Is Bank Receipts confirming that the Beneficiary received
TRY 4,000,000.00 from the sale of his house)
```

**Pattern:** `(Exhibit: [Title] [dated DATE] [— short qualifier])`
Embedded inside narrative, not relegated to footnotes.

### Wire-transfer narration pattern
> "The initial transfer of TRY 30,000.00 was made on October 15, 2025.
> The remaining portion of the deposit TRY 40,000.00 was made by the
> buyer, Feyzullah Guler on October 18, 2025. On the sale day, November
> 24, 2025 the amount of TRY 2,900,000.00, TRY 1,000,000.00, and TRY
> 30,000.00, all credited to the same personal account of Mr. Kacar by
> the buyer Feyzullah Guler."

**Pattern:** date → amount → counterparty → account, in chronological
sequence, named buyer disclosed.

### Defined terms (firm-standard)
- **"the Beneficiary"** — the individual treaty investor (consistent
  throughout, never "Applicant", never "Investor", never first name)
- **"the Petitioner"** — the US enterprise / LLC
- **"Mr./Ms. [Last Name]"** at first formal mention; "the Beneficiary"
  thereafter
- **Currency:** "TRY 4,000,000.00" / "$120,000.00" — explicit ISO code +
  comma-separated thousands + .00 cents

### Citation styles in cover letter
- "INA §101(a)(15)(E)" — single space after § sometimes
- "9 FAM 402.9" — bare reference
- Both cited together: "INA §101(a)(15)(E) and 9 FAM 402.9"

### Cultural / legal context insertion
When an exhibit reflects foreign-jurisdiction practice unfamiliar to
USCIS adjudicators, the firm explains the foreign legal mechanism BEFORE
introducing the exhibit. Example: explaining Tapu Mudurlugu before
introducing the title deed exhibits.

This is a **defensive drafting pattern** — anticipates the RFE
("why is there no purchase contract?") and answers it preemptively.

## Voice features for AI drafter to replicate

| Feature | Pattern |
|---|---|
| Section heads | Roman numeral + ALL CAPS, mirrored to Tab letters |
| Subsection heads | "A.", "B.", "C." + Title Case |
| Defined terms | "the Beneficiary" / "the Petitioner" — consistent |
| Currency | ISO code + commas + .00 always |
| Exhibit cites | Inline parenthetical, never footnote |
| Foreign-law explanation | Insert BEFORE the exhibit, anticipate RFE |
| Wrap-up sentence | Each section ends with "Accordingly, [Element X is satisfied]" pattern |
| Authority | INA + 9 FAM cited together for E-2 |
| Tone | Formal, third-person, no first person, no rhetorical questions |
| Numbers | All written numerals for amounts; words for "fifty percent (50%)" |
